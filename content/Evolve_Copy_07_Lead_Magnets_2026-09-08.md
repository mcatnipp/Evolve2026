# The questions to settle before you sign for the land or the power.

Power may be the first constraint on a data-center site, but it is not the only one, and the decisions you make in the first ninety days control everything after. This checklist puts them in order.

**Primary CTA:** Send Me the Checklist  

### What is inside

- The utility, permitting and site questions that decide whether a site ever energizes, in the order they have to be answered.
- A method for separating what is known from what is assumed before design starts, so assumptions do not become surprises.
- The decision list to carry into your first planning session, with the owner of each decision.
- Where the power position, the site condition and the delivery plan interact, and which one usually moves first.

### Who it is for

Owners and developers comparing sites. Planners preparing a feasibility package. Anyone who has been handed a site and a target capacity and asked how soon it can energize.

### Direct answers

#### Is this a generic checklist?

No. It is built from the site-selection and power-planning questions Evolve works through with owners, organized so each answer feeds the next decision. Every item is a decision or a check, not a topic.

#### Does it cover Texas specifically?

The method applies anywhere in the United States. Where the utility and permitting process differs by region, the checklist tells you which questions to ask locally.

# PAGE-1020 — Modular vs. Traditional Delivery Guide

**URL:** `/resources/modular-vs-traditional-delivery-guide/`  
**Page brief:** INTENT-1020  
**Audience:** Owners, developers and engineers choosing a delivery model  
**Primary action:** Send Me the Guide  

## Publishable page copy

### Hero

**Eyebrow:** Free guide

# Modular or site-built? Compare the interfaces, not the labels, before you choose.

The useful question is not whether modular is better. It is which work belongs in a fabrication shop, which belongs on the site, and how the two meet. This guide gives you the comparison buyers should actually run.

**Primary CTA:** Send Me the Guide  

### What is inside

- Which work belongs in a fabrication shop and which belongs on the site, and why the answer changes with the program.
- The interface questions that decide whether modular saves time or creates rework: pad, power feed, transport, setting and acceptance.
- How to evaluate a modular proposal against a site-built one on equal terms.
- What "factory-built and site-set" should mean in your project conversation.

### Who it is for

Anyone comparing a modular proposal to a site-built one, or deciding whether a distributed or edge program should standardize on fabricated modules.

### Direct answers

#### Does Evolve favor modular delivery?

Evolve self-performs modular fabrication in its own shop and also builds site-constructed facilities. The guide is written to help you decide which work belongs where, not to sell one model.

#### Does the guide include schedule or cost figures?

No. It gives you the comparison method and the interface questions. Schedule and cost depend on the program, and the guide shows you which decisions drive them.

# PAGE-1030 — AI Data Center Planning Brief

**URL:** `/resources/ai-data-center-planning-brief/`  
**Page brief:** INTENT-1030  
**Audience:** Owners, operators and engineers planning AI or HPC capacity  
**Primary action:** Send Me the Brief  

## Publishable page copy

### Hero

**Eyebrow:** Free brief

# The GPU order is the easy part. Settle the facility around it first.

AI programs lose time when the compute assumptions, the power, the cooling and the building are decided by different people at different times. This brief puts the decisions in sequence.

**Primary CTA:** Send Me the Brief  

### What is inside

- The compute and deployment assumptions that drive every downstream facility decision, and how to get them approved.
- Power, thermal, structural and commissioning questions to resolve in order, before design locks.
- How to keep the facility built to the workload instead of to a template.
- The handoff between the compute plan and the construction plan, and who has to own it.

### Who it is for

Anyone responsible for delivering AI or HPC capacity on a date: owners, operators, engineers and the people who have to explain the schedule.

### Direct answers

#### Does the brief specify rack densities or cooling systems?

No. It gives you the sequence of decisions and the questions to ask. Density, cooling and power topology are program-specific, and the brief shows you how to establish them from the workload.

#### Is it relevant for a retrofit rather than a new build?

Yes. The same decision sequence applies when AI density is added to an existing hall; the existing condition becomes the first input.

# PAGE-1050 — Preventive Maintenance Program Guide

**URL:** `/resources/preventive-maintenance-program-guide/`  
**Page brief:** INTENT-1050  
**Audience:** Facility managers, operators and owners responsible for an operating data center  
**Primary action:** Send Me the Guide  

## Publishable page copy

### Hero

**Eyebrow:** Free guide

# A maintenance visit should tell you something. Build the program that makes it.

A completed task list proves a technician showed up. It does not tell you what was observed, what is trending or what decision comes next. This guide shows how to build a program that does.

**Primary CTA:** Send Me the Guide  

### What is inside

- What each maintenance visit should establish and record, beyond "task complete."
- How to tie the program to the equipment you actually run: current condition, operating requirements and documented responsibilities.
- The reporting that turns findings into budget, replacement and risk decisions.
- How to evaluate a maintenance proposal, whether it comes from an OEM, an independent or Evolve.

### Who it is for

Facility and operations managers, owners of operating data centers, and anyone renewing or replacing a maintenance agreement.

### Direct answers

#### Does the guide include service intervals or checklists by equipment type?

It gives you the program structure and the questions each visit should answer. Intervals depend on the equipment, the environment and the manufacturer's requirements, and the guide shows how to set them from those inputs.

#### Is this only for Evolve-maintained facilities?

No. The program structure applies to any operating facility. If you want Evolve to run the program, the request-service page routes the discussion.

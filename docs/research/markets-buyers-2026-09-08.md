## 2. Buyer journey and search behavior

**Cross-segment behavior (Fact).** First vendor contact comes about 61% of the way through the journey; 94% of buying groups rank a shortlist of about 3.6 names before engaging, four in five deals go to the pre-contact favorite, and 94% use LLMs while buying (6sense, 2025 [S46]). 67% of B2B buyers prefer a rep-free experience and 45% used AI in a recent purchase (Gartner, Mar. 2026 [S47]). Among software buyers, 51% now start in an AI chatbot rather than Google and 69% switched vendors on chatbot guidance (G2, Apr. 15, 2026 [S48]); no construction equivalent exists. Segment pages must answer shortlist questions before anyone calls.

**Questions asked before shortlisting (Fact, from published checklists and RFPs).** MW delivered in three years, schedule and budget record, EMR below 1.0, live-data-hall protocols, bonding, insurance, ISO 9001, regional presence, labor availability, sample integrated-systems-test reports [S49]; Basis of Design before the RFP, named standards, operations input, independent commissioning, stage documentation (Uptime [S51]); build model, workforce and training partnerships, long-lead de-risking, community-engagement evidence, capex benchmarks (Stargate [S52]); certification renewal dates, SLA specificity, access logs, incident notification, generator runtime (financial services [S15]); responsiveness and responsibility tests, references, 25% MBE and 5% WBE goals, site visits (public agency, MPEA, Aug. 8, 2024 [S53]).

## 4. Blind spots on the review site

1. **Seven verticals held with no landing content** (healthcare, financial, telecom, government and education, energy and industrial, edge market page, crypto). Every competitor above publishes vertical pages and the mockup's own navigation carries seven industries [S61]; rep-free shortlists find no fit signal. Active pursuit of each is RQ-010.
2. **The cooperative route is invisible.** Contract 25/033MR-15 is public [S19], shown on the current site and removed under CLM-038 [S63]. Public buyers who could piggyback in weeks instead of 6–9 months [S24] get no page, number or steps; DataVox and M.C. Dean show the page type.
3. **Crypto-to-AI conversions, the largest retrofit wave in Evolve's home state** [S41][S42][S44], are unnamed on Brownfield & Retrofit and the segment is held.
4. **Neoclouds and their landlords are not addressed** despite 18% of demand [S1] and multi-GW pipelines [S10].
5. **No compliance framing.** The ledger bars credential claims, not explanation. Nothing shows a hospital how 72-hour restoration [S13] shapes design, a bank how 100-mile separation and generator runtime [S15] enter the Basis of Design, a sheriff's office what CJIS 6.0 (Oct. 1, 2027) means for a facility [S23], or a Texas owner what SB 6 site-control duties [S38] mean for Plan.
6. **Reference-ability stops at "No."** The colocation FAQ answers the references question with a bare "No"; buyers ask for references and sample IST reports [S49][S53]. Competitors show confidential-client projects [S60]; Evolve's stricter rule still permits a process statement.
7. **No prequalification packet.** Bonding, insurance, EMR, ISO status and delivery models are absent; Clune leads with EMR/TRIR/LTIR/DART. The mockup shows EMR .77 and "zero injuries since 2004," conflicting with the approved "since 2010" [S61][S63].
8. **No partner or channel path.** The contact form routes "Vendor or subcontractor," but no page speaks to engineers of record, brokers and site advisors, or GCs needing a modular fabricator; Salute lists General Contractor as a segment, Vertiv runs consultant programs, JE Dunn and Gilbane publish trade-partner programs.
9. **"Nationwide" with no regional evidence.** Buyers weigh regional presence and labor availability [S49]; competitors list offices; Houston is only an address and ERCOT Batch Zero [S37] goes unmentioned (see companion power and service reports).
10. **Careers held while workforce is a buying criterion.** Over half of operators struggle to hire [S7]; construction needs 349,000 workers in 2026 with electrician demand tied to data centers (ABC, Jan. 15, 2026 [S55]); shortages stay acute as data centers strain supply (AGC/NCCER, Sep. 3, 2026 [S57]); data-center wages run up to 30% above standard construction and about 20,000 electricians retire yearly (iRecruit, Aug. 31, 2026 [S56]); Stargate requires workforce plans [S52]. Turner, Salute and M.C. Dean sell veteran and trade pathways.
11. **Community and sustainability absent as positions.** 70% of Americans oppose a local AI data center (Gallup, May 13, 2026 [S58]); 75+ projects worth about $130B were blocked or delayed in Q1 2026 [S59]; Stargate asks for community-engagement evidence; Gilbane puts community engagement on its segment page. The review site has one insight and no stance.
12. **Intake ignores segment and route.** Fields cover interest, stage, state and summary; nothing captures industry, role, cooperative membership, RFP versus negotiated award or budget status.
13. **Proof conflicts remain live.** The mockup shows client logos (Holder, DHL, Green Bank, Dow, Foxconn), 1,000+/110+/52 facility counts and 24/7 claims the ledger blocks [S61][S63]; segment pages built from mockup copy would republish them.
14. **Public-sector page types missing:** no contract-vehicle page, JOC explainer, federal-lands or installation-resilience content [S28][S30][S32][S33].
